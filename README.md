# ExpenseRecord — Static Personal Expense & Credit Tracker PWA (₹)

**Track every rupee. Stay on budget.**

A 100% static, offline-first Progressive Web App (PWA) for tracking personal expenses, credits, and monthly budgets in Indian Rupees (`₹`). There is **no backend server, no database server, no environment variables, no login, and no API calls**—open the link and use it immediately. All data lives privately on your phone in **IndexedDB** (with automatic `localStorage` fallback) and persists across sessions.

---

## How to Install on Your Phone Home Screen

### Android (Chrome)
1. Open the app URL in **Google Chrome** on your Android phone.
2. Tap the **📲 Install** button in the top bar of the app, **or** tap the Chrome menu (**⋮** in the top-right corner) and select **Install app** / **Add to Home screen**.
3. Confirm by tapping **Install**. The **ExpenseRecord** icon will appear on your Android home screen and launch full-screen offline.

### iPhone / iPad (Safari)
1. Open the app URL in **Safari** on your iPhone or iPad.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an upward arrow at the bottom of the screen).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**.
4. Tap **Add** in the top-right corner. **ExpenseRecord** will appear on your home screen with its custom icon.
